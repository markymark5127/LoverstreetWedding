Meet Katelyn

A gentle presence for one of the biggest moments of your life.

Hello and welcome. I am an authorized marriage celebrant in the State of Virginia, based in Forest. Since launching Loverstreet Wedding Ceremonies in February 2022, I have performed more than 80 weddings — and I still love bringing couples together in matrimony on their special day.

Couples tell me they chose me for a kind spirit, clear communication, and a process that feels easy instead of stressful. Whether you want a simple, legal “I do,” an intimate elopement, or a ceremony woven with the story of how you met, I will meet you there.

I can’t wait to meet you and your significant other.
